How We Teach

Less Theory. More Doing.

We believe the best way to learn a skill is to use it. Our learning approach is built around five steps:

01

LEARN

Understand the concepts through instructor-led classroom sessions.

02

EXPLORE

Get familiar with relevant AI tools, platforms, and technologies.

03

PRACTICE

Complete assignments and work on practical exercises.

04

BUILD

Create real projects and develop a portfolio that demonstrates your abilities.

05

GROW

Receive feedback, mentorship, career guidance, and support as you progress.

By the end of your learning journey, you should have more than knowledge. You should have practical work that demonstrates what you can do.

You’re Never on Your Own.

Learning something new can be challenging. Having the right guidance makes a difference. At SPARK, your learning journey includes one year of mentorship and continued learning support.

Whether you’re struggling with an assignment, exploring an AI tool, improving a project, or figuring out your next career move — you’ll have access to guidance along the way.

  • One-on-one learning sessions
  • Live Q&A and dedicated chat support
  • Your progress matters — not just your attendance
