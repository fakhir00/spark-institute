What To Expect

Before You Enroll, Know This.

  • No prior experience needed. You don’t need to be a tech expert — you need the willingness to learn.
  • Learn in person. Live, instructor-led classroom sessions with real interaction — not recorded videos alone.
  • Small batches, real attention. Limited enrollment means your instructor actually knows your name and your progress.
  • Build from week one. Assignments, real tools, and projects start early — you graduate with a portfolio, not just notes.
  • Support for a full year. Mentorship, Q&A, and learning support continue long after your last class.
